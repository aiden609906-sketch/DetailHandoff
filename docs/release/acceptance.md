## Performance evidence and budgets

CI `34560087867`, attempt 2, recorded the 40-photo PDF regression at 2.984 seconds with a 2,486,430-byte, 38-page output. The complete in-memory backup roundtrip took 0.446 seconds, and disk-store replacement plus fresh-context verification took 0.412 seconds. Engineering guardrails for this deterministic simulator fixture are 10 seconds and 10 MB for the 40-photo PDF, and 5 seconds each for backup roundtrip and disk restore. The observed results are inside those guardrails. They are not substitutes for peak-memory measurement or large real-photo testing on the supported physical devices; those remain release gates.
